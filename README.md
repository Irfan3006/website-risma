# RISMA Al-Falaah Karangjambe — Official Website

The official website of **RISMA Al-Falaah Karangjambe**, an Islamic youth organization based in Karangjambe, Banguntapan, Yogyakarta.

This website serves as a digital platform for sharing information, activities, and documentation related to Islamic learning, community development, and youth empowerment.

**Visit the website:** [https://risma-alfalaah.vercel.app/](https://risma-alfalaah.vercel.app/)

---

## About RISMA

**RISMA (Remaja Islam Masjid Al-Falaah)** is an Islamic youth organization under the guidance of Masjid Al-Falaah Karangjambe.

RISMA Al-Falaah Karangjambe aims to strengthen faith, build brotherhood, and encourage young people to take an active role in religious, educational, social, and community activities.

Some of RISMA’s programs include:

- Islamic study sessions and Qur’an learning
- Social activities such as Zakat, Infaq, and Shadaqah
- Ramadan programs and community gatherings
- Youth creativity, leadership, and contribution development

## Website Features

- **Home Page** to introduce RISMA and its mission
- **Activity Information** to display community agendas and programs
- **Gallery** to document past activities
- **Location and Contact** with Google Maps integration
- **Responsive Design** for smooth access across different devices
- **Modern Interface** with simple animations and a calm Islamic atmosphere

---

## Technologies Used

- **HTML5** and **CSS3**
- **Bootstrap** for responsive layout
- **AOS (Animate On Scroll)** for transition animations
- **Vercel** for fast and stable hosting

---

## Purpose of the Website

This website was created to:

- Make RISMA activity information easier to access online
- Improve communication between RISMA members and the community
- Provide a publication platform for religious and social activities
- Encourage young people to actively participate in mosque programs
- Support the digitalization of Islamic youth organizations in Karangjambe
